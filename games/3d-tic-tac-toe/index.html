Tic Tac Toe in a cube: four layers of 4 × 4 cells. First to get four in a row wins, also across the layers and diagonally through the whole cube.

Ways to play

  • Against the AI · 4 levels
  • Two players on one device
  • Online with friends

Runs in the browser on phones and computers, no download and no sign-up needed to start.

How to play

The goal

You play in a cube of four layers with 4 × 4 cells each. You take turns placing a stone in any free cell, in any layer. First to get four in a row wins.

Lines in one layer

As in Tic Tac Toe, lines count horizontally, vertically and diagonally within a layer.

Lines through the layers

The same cell in all four layers is a line too, and so are staircases: one cell further per layer, straight or diagonal. The four long diagonals run through the whole cube, corner to corner.

Views

Above the board you switch the view. Front and Side slice the cube differently, so lines through the layers look completely normal. 3D stacks the layers on top of each other, and you can turn the cube.

Tips

The Corners and the four middle cells of the two inner layers lie on the most lines. If the opponent has three in a row with one free cell, Versus marks that cell with a red dashed line (can be turned off with the hints).
