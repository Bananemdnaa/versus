## English (en-us)

**Product name:** Versus

**Short description:**

> 39 games for two to four players: Chess, Ludo, Mau-Mau, Battleship, Light Racer and many more. Play together on one PC, against the AI in four levels, or online against friends. Free, no ads, works offline.

**Description:**

> Versus is a game collection for quick matches between friends. One PC, two to four people, go.
>
> ON ONE DEVICE
> Sit down in front of one screen and take turns. In Air Hockey, Pong or Light Racer you play at the same time on one keyboard.
>
> AGAINST THE AI
> Almost every board and card game has a computer opponent in four levels, from Easy to Pro. "Matched to you" sets the AI to your strength, and your AI Elo shows how good you really are.
>
> ONLINE WITH FRIENDS
> Add friends, challenge them or send a link to a table. Quick match finds an opponent right away. Online matches count for the leaderboard.
>
> THE GAMES
> Classics: Connect 4, Battleship, Tic Tac Toe (also Ultimate, 3D and Hardcore), Five in a Row, Dots and Boxes, Hangman, Code Breaker, Who Am I?, Mine Hunt
> Board games: Chess, Checkers, Nine Men's Morris, Go, Reversi, Backgammon, Ludo, Snakes and Ladders, Kalah, Hex, Blockade
> Cards and dice: Mau-Mau, Schnapsen, Poker (no real money), Dominoes, Memory, Dice Block
> Party: City Country River, Alphabet Game, Estimation Quiz, Draw & Guess
> Action: Air Hockey, Pong, Light Racer, Sumo Arena, Pool, Reaction Duel
>
> MADE FOR THE PC
> Sidebar, move list next to the board, keyboard shortcuts for everything and drag and drop with the mouse. The same app runs on your phone too, and a free account takes your record everywhere.
>
> No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking. Most games work without internet.

**What's new:** Versus is now in the Microsoft Store.

**Product features:**

1. 39 games for 2 to 4 players

3. AI opponents in four levels, optionally matched to your strength
4. Online against friends, with quick match and leaderboard
5. Keyboard shortcuts and mouse controls in every game
6. Many games work offline
7. Free, no ads and no in-app purchases
